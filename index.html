About me

I’ve been interested in computers from an early age, which gradually led me to programming. I started by learning Python, HTML, and CSS on my own, and later began working with C# through Unity, where I developed my first game projects and became more familiar with application logic and object-oriented programming.

After joining IT STEP Academy in Dnipro, Ukraine, I began studying C# and the .NET ecosystem in greater depth, along with SQL, databases, and C++. This gave me a stronger technical foundation and allowed me to move from smaller educational projects to more complete applications.

With this experience, I started developing my own projects, including CoffeeNap and MusicStore. Working on them introduced me to many practical challenges involving architecture, databases, application logic, performance, and maintainability. Solving these problems became one of the most valuable parts of my learning.

My largerst project is CoffeeNap, a caffeine tracking application built with .NET MAUI and SQLite. I developed its internal logic, navigation, data handling, local database integration, caffeine calculation system, localization logic, and other application functionality. More details about the challenges I faced and the decisions I made during development can be found on the individual project pages.

Today, my main focus is C# .NET and backend-oriented software development.
